Control your Samsung air conditioner from Homey without the cloud. A small ESPHome bridge on the indoor unit's F1/F2 bus talks to the air conditioner directly, and this app gives you one complete Homey device per indoor unit, with energy in Homey Energy.
Unofficial community project, not affiliated with Samsung. You need a bridge made of an M5Stack ATOM Lite on the ATOM RS485 base, running the Samsung HVAC Local firmware (ESPHome).
